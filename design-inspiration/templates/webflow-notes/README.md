## Strongest recurring premium patterns across the set

1. **Scale over weight** — headlines are huge (52-115px) but weight 400-600, never 800+.
2. **One accent max** — every template is dominant-neutral + ink + a single accent (gold, blue, cyan, blush) or no accent at all.
3. **Text-first heroes** — 6 of 8 have no hero photo in the initial viewport; imagery arrives below the fold or as a side collage.
4. **Whitespace as luxury** — 100-160px section padding and deliberately empty hero bands.
5. **↗ arrow glyphs** on links/buttons as the signature "designed" micro-detail.
6. **Tinted band sections** (pale blue/warm gray) instead of hard color flips for rhythm; dark bands reserved for one or two "statement" sections.
7. **Restrained motion** — fade-up on scroll + tiny hover transforms; the most expensive-looking templates animate the least.
8. **List/index rows with hairline rules** (clients, services, work) instead of card grids — reads editorial and is trivially GHL-safe.
