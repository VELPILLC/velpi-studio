# Professional palettes — by niche and mood

Sources: working palettes extracted from the collected templates (BootstrapMade CSS
variables, HTML5 UP stylesheets, Colorlib bs6 `--brand` tokens, Webflow demo computed
styles — see `../templates/`) plus standard 60/30/10 color practice. Colorlib oklch
brands are converted to approximate hex (marked ≈).

Format per palette:
- **60% dominant** (page ground) / **30% secondary** (ink + surfaces) / **10% accent** (CTAs, links, highlights)
- **Dark section:** the variant for a dark band/footer on the same site
- One line on when it fits.

Rules that hold across every observed premium template:
- Accent appears on interactive elements and small highlights ONLY — never as a section background wider than a band.
- Dark sections reuse the same accent (or a lightened version of it), never a new hue.
- Body ink is almost never pure black on white — `#141618`–`#333` over white, or off-white `#F2F4F6` over dark.

## Anti-patterns observed in the dated templates
- Accent used as full hero background (Creativo's green) — reads 2015.
- Two unrelated accents (gradient cyan #9BF1FF→#53E3FB in Forty) — pick one stop.
- Pure #000 text on pure #FFF everywhere — harsh; every premium observed site softens one side.
- Neon-mint dominants (Paradigm Shift #49FCD4) — fine for a poster, fatiguing as 60%.
