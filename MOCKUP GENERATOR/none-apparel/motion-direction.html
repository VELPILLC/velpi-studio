The idea

We make
very little.

NONE started as a subtraction. We took a wardrobe apart and kept removing until only the things that got worn every week were left. Three of them. That is the whole collection, and it is meant to stay that way.

Everything is cut generously and finished plainly. No seasonal colour, no drop schedule, no logo doing the talking. When a piece wears out we will make it again the same way, in the same cotton, from the same mill outside Porto.

It is a quieter way to get dressed. You stop deciding, and the morning gets shorter.

Fewer things, kept longer, in colours borrowed from the ground and the sky.
